01The teams

I started as an assistant coach for the middle school boys' volleyball, girls' volleyball, and girls' basketball teams at Tsinghua International School, and in my senior year became head coach of girls' volleyball and girls' basketball, all while playing as a full-time student-athlete.

02What I did

I ran practices, set lineups, gave individual feedback and advice, tracked and analyzed statistics, and managed rosters across multiple teams.

03Results

Both placements came with the girls' basketball team while I was an assistant coach, before my senior year: second in ISAC U14 Division III in 2020–21, and third in Division IV in 2022–23.

04What it taught me

Coaching is where I first learned to lead, and to break something complicated into small pieces. A play only works once every kid knows their own part in it, so I'd teach it one piece at a time and then put it back together. I still learn and explain engineering that way.

It also taught me to plan for things going wrong, because they always did. Almost no practice or game went the way I'd drawn it up, so I came in with backups and changed things on the fly.
