01The job

I've been a teaching assistant for EGR 105, Computing for Engineers, since fall 2025, and I grade for ME 221, Structure and Properties of Solids. I have a section of 39 students in each course. In EGR 105, students come in with very different backgrounds, and the work spans coding projects, lab work, mini-activities, and problem sets. In ME 221, my job is grading.

02What I do

In EGR 105, I hold weekly office hours, lead lab sections, and run one-on-one sessions, and I review student projects, quizzes, and lab work. In ME 221, I grade.

03How I teach

I break hard concepts down into their fundamentals and teach the process instead of supplying answers. When I review EGR 105 work, I give feedback, suggest more efficient approaches where they exist, and recommend specific changes for resubmission.

04What it's taught me

Teaching has taught me a lot of the same things coaching did, mostly how to lead and how to break a hard idea down into its basics. When a student is stuck, giving them the answer almost never helps. Finding the one basic idea they're missing usually does, and I've started working through my own problems the same way.
