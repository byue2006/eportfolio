01The role

I'm a teaching assistant for two courses: EGR 105, Computing for Engineers, for two years, and ME 221, Structure and Properties of Solids, for one, with two sections of 39 students each. Students come in with very different backgrounds, and the work spans coding projects, lab work, mini-activities, and problem sets.

02My contribution

I hold weekly office hours, lead lab sections, and run individual instructional sessions. I also advise on student projects, quizzes, and lab work.

03Approach

I break hard concepts down into their fundamentals and teach the process instead of supplying answers. When I review submitted work, I give feedback, suggest more efficient approaches where they exist, and recommend specific changes for resubmission.
