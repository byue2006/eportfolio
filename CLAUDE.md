## How it's built
- All page text lives in `tools/build_site.py`. Edit there, then run `python3 tools/build_site.py`; never hand-edit the generated `*.html`.
- Personal gallery: photos go in `personal/<category>/`; `python3 tools/build_gallery.py` (needs Pillow, pillow-heif) writes `personal/_web/` and `personal/gallery.js` (captions/alt edited there persist). Category text: `personal/categories.js` (strict JSON).
- 3D models and video: `python3 tools/build_media.py <file> <slug>` (CAD: STEP/STL/OBJ/GLB/zip, `--up y` for SolidWorks, `--finish aluminum`; video: `--loop` for silent b-roll, `--start/--end`) writes `media/<slug>.glb|.mp4` + `.webp` poster, ≤24 MB, and `tools/media.json`. Needs `pip install cascadio trimesh`. Place on a page with `model3d('slug', alt, num, cap, hotspots=[...])` or `video('slug', ..., loop=True)` / `video(None, ..., yt='ID')`; `lead=True` makes it the page's hero.
- CAD colors are converted to linear and made satin plastic by `build_media.py` (CAD exports have no PBR values, and glTF would otherwise render them as washed-out metal). The 3D viewer and the poster renderer both light models with `assets/studio.hdr` (a product-photo studio made by `tools/make_studio_env.py`: key, fill, rim and top softboxes on black) so edges, lettering and ports read. Current builds: closure `--finish aluminum`, shuttle `--finish white --yaw 180 --orbit "38deg 52deg auto"` (so VANGUARD and DU/KE read), train head and balance keep CAD colors. The shear-balance model is recolored by part after building: `python3 tools/recolor_balance.py media/shear-balance.glb`, then re-render its poster with `tools/media_poster.js`. CAD originals are not kept in the repo (they are in git history and outside the repo).
- Home order: Experience, Projects (both under "Selected work"), Leadership, then Smaller work (its own section, `#smaller`), then the About teaser. Strongest material first; Smaller work is the bonus at the end.
- Keeping it light (Nov 2026; nothing may look different):
  - On-page images use per-page copies in `images/d/` sized to what each page needs (on-screen size x2 desktop, x3 phone). Pipeline after adding images or changing layouts: serve the site locally, `node tools/measure_images.js http://localhost:8000/ > tools/display_widths.json`, `python3 tools/make_display_images.py`, `python3 tools/build_site.py`. `build_site.py` swaps `<img src>`/mask URLs per page from `tools/display_map.json`; links (the lightbox) keep the full originals, which are never modified. Images without a copy just use the original.
  - 3D models are meshopt-compressed (`python3 tools/compress_models.py`, run LAST: after build_media.py, recolor_balance.py, which needs the uncompressed file, and media_poster.js). The viewer decodes with the self-hosted `assets/vendor/meshopt_decoder.js` and its library loads only when a model comes within ~700px of the screen (the poster shows until then).
  - `assets/studio.hdr` is RLE-compressed (lossless) by `make_studio_env.py`.
  - The personal gallery's 900px thumbnails are already what phones (3x) and cropped desktop tiles need; don't shrink them.
- Logo files (favicon.svg/.ico, apple-touch-icon.png, images/og-card.png, assets/brand/) all come from `python3 tools/make_brand.py` (outlines stored in the script; PNGs rendered by `tools/brand_png.js`). The header draws the same mark inline (`BRAND_MARK` in build_site.py; colors are the `--mark-*` tokens in site.css). The share card headline mirrors the home hero, so update both together. QR codes for events come from `python3 tools/make_qr.py` (needs `pip install segno`; pass another URL as an argument to point one elsewhere): `assets/brand/qr-brianyue.svg|-4096.png` (BY mark in the middle), `qr-brianyue-plain.*` (code alone, the most forgiving for small print) and `qr-phone-card.png` (a phone-shaped screen to hold up, 3510 x 7596: Brian asked for it "a lot more HD"). Always dark squares on white with the four-square white margin left clear; after any change, check that the files still read (the script's layout was chosen by a scan stress test, see its docstring).
- Project banners show a small single-color organization mark (`org_mark()`; the logo is a CSS mask in the ink color, sizes in `LOGO_AR`/`LOGO_SCALE`).
- Smaller work: the `SMALL` list in `build_site.py` feeds both `work/smaller-work.html` and the "Smaller work" card row on the home page (anchors per entry). Those cards are dark, pointer-reactive posters in the ISS style (`anim_iss`, `anim_snap`, `anim_vanguard` in `build_site.py`; CSS under "animated card posters" in `site.css`; physics in the last block of `site.js`): an RK4 orbit with drag, a spring snap-fit the cursor pushes, and a shuttle with three plumes (nozzle positions in `VANGUARD_NOZZLES`, measured from the model for `images/card-vanguard.webp`; re-measure if that render changes). Still at rest, live on hover/focus, on screen on touch devices, never under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Search engines, link previews and AI tools (Nov 2026; all invisible to visitors): `robots.txt` allows everything and points at `sitemap.xml`; every page has a unique title, description, canonical URL and share-card tags; `build_site.py` also writes schema.org data into each page head (`PERSON`, `project_schema()`: Person + WebSite on home, ProfilePage on About, Article + breadcrumbs on project pages, the AIAA paper as a ScholarlyArticle with its DOI) and `llms.txt`, a plain-text index of the whole site built from the card text. All page text is in the HTML and readable without JavaScript; keep it that way (no content that only appears after scripts run). Facts in the structured data must also be stated on the visible page.
- Link previews: home, About, Personal and Smaller work show the main share card (`images/og-card.png`); each project page shows its own `images/og/<slug>.jpg` (same look, with the project's picture in a tile). They are drawn by `python3 tools/make_share_cards.py` from `tools/share_cards.json`, which `build_site.py` writes; `build_site.py` prints a NOTE when a card is out of date (after renaming a project or changing its card picture): run the card script, then `build_site.py` again. Per-card picture choices live in `PICK` in that script (Happy Meal uses the wider photo so all three heads show; ISSDC shows the competition logo because every robot render is a wide strip; coaching is cropped to the tactics board so no faces show).
- Résumé: the file in the repository is `resume.pdf` (that address keeps working for old links). Every build copies it to `Brian-Yue-Resume.pdf`, which is what the site links to, so a download has his name on it. To update the résumé, replace `resume.pdf` only.
- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs both scripts on every push and commits results back to `main`.
- `.assetsignore` keeps tools, READMEs, inbox, originals and stray root uploads off the live site. New top-level files that should NOT be public must be added there.
- Never put `[skip ci]` in a commit message (Cloudflare skips the deploy too).

## Voice (Brian asked for the site to feel human-made, not AI-written)
- Write the way Brian writes in his cover letters and essays: first person, plain sentences, specific details, a little personality ("which I'll admit is a strange way to fall in love with airplanes"). Contractions are fine.
- Action-oriented and direct (Brian, Nov 2026): lead with the verb for what he did, "I designed", "I modeled", "I ran the FEA", "we delivered", never "I did the design for", "the design was mine", "was delivered", "ended up", "worked on". His résumé bullets are the model. Directness never upgrades a claim: "we" stays "we", "most of" stays "most of", and every credit to someone else stays.
- Avoid the tells: slogan or aphorism lines ("the drawing is the design"), "X, not Y" reversals, tidy rule-of-three lists, colon-reveal summaries ("Thing: detail, detail"), "@" in titles, decorative drafting labels.
- The home page scrolling ticker lists tools and skills (Brian: scrolling is for secondary info, because people glaze over it and it never shows everything at once). It has a fixed "Tools & skills" label, muted items and grey "+" separators, and doesn't pause on hover, so it never reads as links. Numbers are important, so they are never in a ticker: each project page shows its key figures as a still grid under the banner (`NUMS` in `build_site.py`, `.stats.nums`; same box style as the coaching stats). Every figure there must be stated on that page.
- Section headings are specific to each project (e.g. "Merging the ports", "The Q&A"), not one template.
- His origin story (The Martian at ten, Mayday: Air Crash Investigation, "watching something I drew turn into a part that works") comes from his own cover letters and essays and is fine to use.
