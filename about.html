About

Beijing, then Durham.

I'm a mechanical engineering junior at Duke's Pratt School of Engineering, working toward a certificate in aerospace engineering. Before Duke I was at Tsinghua International School in Beijing, where I captained our ISSDC teams and, as a full-time student-athlete, coached middle school volleyball and basketball, ending as head coach of both girls' teams.

At Duke I split my time between research, teaching, and Duke Aero's solid propulsion subteam, which I joined in August 2024.

I got into space through The Martian when I was ten. What stuck with me wasn't the rescue, it was all the hardware that made it possible. Airplanes came from Mayday: Air Crash Investigation, which I'll admit is a strange way to fall in love with them, but every episode took an aircraft apart and explained how it worked. These days the part I enjoy most is watching something I drew turn into a part that works.

Outside engineering, I play trombone in the Duke University Marching Band, and I play volleyball and basketball.

I'm a native speaker of English and Mandarin, and a US citizen.
